Hudson privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Hudson is an independent app that shows PATH train times and alerts. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey.

In short: Hudson does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app does over the internet

To show train times and alerts, the app downloads two public data files from the Port Authority's website, www.panynj.gov. These requests carry no information about you beyond what any web request carries (for example your IP address), and they go directly from your phone to the Port Authority, whose own privacy policy applies to them. Hudson has no server of its own.

Location

If you tap “Find stations near me” and allow it, the app reads your phone's location while the Stations screen is open, to work out which PATH stations are closest. Android lets you choose precise or approximate; approximate is enough. This happens entirely on your phone. Your location is never stored, never sent anywhere and never used in the background. You can use every other part of the app without allowing it, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in Android's settings.

What is stored on your phone

Your saved stations and settings, and the most recent copy of the train times and alerts (so the app and its widget can show something when you have no signal). This stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

If you start “Track on lock screen”, the app shows a notification with the next trains for the station you chose. It is created on your phone; nothing is sent to a notification service.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.
